01-solar-horizon-glacial-cyan

Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: widescreen desktop wallpaper colour variant.
Input images: Image 1 is the edit target. It is an already composed wallpaper; its framing and all visual forms are locked.
Primary request: Apply only the following colour treatment to the attached wallpaper: A cool glacial cyan treatment: deepest coloured shadows are midnight blue and petrol teal; midtones are rich ocean blue and turquoise; fine bright filaments and corona are icy cyan with sparing pale blue-white highlights. The original black regions remain pure black. Entirely cool, with no orange or yellow. Refined luminous texture, not excessively neon.
Composition invariants: Keep the broad shallow solar horizon in the lower half, cropped beyond both sides and bottom, the black upper half, and the locations of every dark surface feature and luminous loop.
Preservation: Keep exactly the original landscape aspect ratio, crop, scale, silhouette, negative space, fine plasma texture, dark coronal holes, corona structure, and original luminance hierarchy. This is a colour grade of the existing picture. Match its intricate organic solar filaments as closely as possible.
Polish: Smooth colour transitions, deep clean black, clear shadow detail, controlled bright highlights, no banding. Preserve the original sharpness and photographic character.
Constraints: One finished edge-to-edge wallpaper. No new objects, new plasma features, starfield, labels, text, borders, montage, mockup or lens flare. Do not alter the composition or turn the plasma into terrestrial terrain or generic flames.
